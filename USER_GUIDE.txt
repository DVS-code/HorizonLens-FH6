HorizonLens 1.0.0
=================

Use your phone as a hand-held camera inside Forza Horizon 6, with the game
streamed live to the phone and recordings saved on your PC.

- Car View presets (Passenger, Driver, Window, Bumper, Wheel, GAP) and a
  Freecam you walk around with the stick, height control and zoom.
- Position tracking: Android phones with ARCore, and the iPhone app (ARKit).
- Gap Cam: Static (put the phone down and drive), Dynamic (zooms out with
  speed) and Player Tracking (follows other players near you).
- Lock for dolly shots, an on-screen zoom slider and a Hide button for a
  clean screen.
- Recording on the PC with game sound, up to the game's full resolution.
- A cinematic sequencer inside the game, air suspension and a Forza Vista
  remote.

HorizonLens is a fan-made tool. It is not made, endorsed or supported by
Microsoft, Xbox Game Studios or Playground Games. It loads a camera module
into the game to move the camera: use it at your own risk.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- Forza Horizon 6 on Steam or the Microsoft Store (Xbox app / Game Pass)
- A phone on the same Wi-Fi as the PC: iPhone (Safari) or Android (Chrome)
- Any recent NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card. NVIDIA sends the game
  straight from its GPU frames; AMD and Intel use window capture.

SET UP
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (for example your Desktop). Keep every file
   together; HorizonLens.exe needs the _internal folder next to it.
2. Run Allow-Firewall.bat once (it asks for administrator rights). This
   lets your phone reach the PC. Your Wi-Fi must be set to Private in
   Windows Settings > Network & internet.
3. Run HorizonLens.exe. If Windows SmartScreen appears, choose
   More info > Run anyway.

   Antivirus: HorizonLens loads a small camera module into the game, which
   some antivirus tools flag. If HorizonLens.exe or files in _internal
   disappear, add the HorizonLens folder as an exclusion and unzip again.

USE IT
1. Start Forza Horizon 6 (or press Launch game) and get into free roam.
   Only the game is captured, never your desktop.
2. Press Start. A QR code appears. HorizonLens takes the game camera on its
   own once you're driving; "Camera control" turns green.
3. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera. The first time, the browser
   warns about the certificate: on iPhone tap Show Details, then visit this
   website; on Android tap Advanced, then Proceed.
4. Tap Tap to Start and allow Motion & Orientation access. On Android,
   also allow the camera for AR when Chrome asks (see POSITION TRACKING).
5. Point the phone. Tap the round arrow (bottom right) to recenter.

ON THE PHONE
- CAR VIEW: Passenger, Driver, Window, Bumper, Wheel and GAP (a fishbowl
  camera behind the car) views. Move a view with the stick (it walks toward
  where the phone points) and HEIGHT, then tap SAVE to keep it.
- GAP: pinch out past 0.5x for an extra-wide view. Each wide step takes a
  second to switch.
- Settings > Gap Cam > Static Gap Cam: GAP locks where it points, so you can
  put the phone down and drive. The stick and HEIGHT still move it; Recenter
  points it straight ahead. A STATIC badge shows while it's on: tap it (or
  "Lock to where the phone points now" in Settings) to lock it again where
  the phone points, without switching it off and on.
- Dynamic Gap Cam (appears under Static Gap Cam): the lens widens with speed,
  easing smoothly. By default it keeps your zoom up to 100 mph and reaches
  0.3x of it at 250 mph; "Starts at" and "Zoom out to" change both. Past 0.5x
  the camera pulls back instead (the game can't go wider). Pinch zoom sets
  the parked zoom.
- Player Tracking (appears under Static Gap Cam): the locked camera turns to
  follow the nearest other player driving near you, then eases back to where
  you locked it. AI traffic and parked cars are ignored. Best in an online
  session. "Track cars within" sets how close a car must get (5 to 200 m,
  60 m by default); Smoothness sets how gently the camera turns. The STATIC
  badge shows which car it follows and how far away it is.
- FREECAM: walk around the car with the stick and HEIGHT.
- Zoom: pinch, or drag the zoom slider at the side of the screen (double-tap
  it for 1x).
- Lock (padlock button): the camera holds its place and direction, so
  moving, turning or walking with the phone no longer moves it; the stick
  and HEIGHT still move it in a straight line, for dolly and slider shots. A LOCKED badge
  shows; tap it to re-aim where the phone points, Recenter points it
  straight ahead, tap the padlock again to unlock.
- The ... button: zoom, zoom slider, Run, Steadycam, Drone Inertia, motion
  effects (Walking adds a realistic walking step in Freecam).
- The red button records on the PC (in Videos\HorizonLens), with game sound,
  showing exactly what the phone shows. The photos button lists and downloads
  recordings. Settings > Recording > Quality: Standard records the live
  view; High (1080x1920, 24 Mbps) and Max (the game's full resolution,
  50 Mbps) record the same shot through their own capture on the PC, much
  sharper, without touching the live view. Portrait recordings are a 9:16
  slice of the game screen, so a higher game resolution (or NVIDIA DSR /
  DLDSR) gives them more real detail.
- Hide button: Settings > Appearance > Hide Button adds an eye button (top
  left). Tap it to hide every control so only the picture is left; tap it
  again to bring them back.

CINEMATIC SEQUENCER (in-game menu)
- Desktop app > Sequencer > "Open in-game sequencer". The menu appears inside
  Forza's picture. Forza must NOT be in fullscreen: in Forza open
  Settings > Video and turn Fullscreen off (HorizonLens shows a warning card
  if it is on). F8 opens / closes the menu. HorizonLens recordings never
  include it; for OBS or screenshots use "Hide for shot".
- No camera control yet? Press "Take camera" in the menu (it presses Start
  for you if HorizonLens isn't started).
- The menu never takes focus from Forza, so the game keeps drawing behind
  it. To type in a field, click it, type, press Enter (Esc cancels). Your
  keys go to the field, not the car, until then.
- Closing the HorizonLens window closes the sequencer too and gives the
  camera back to the game.
- The menu shows what to do next in orange under the timeline, and what
  the camera is doing at the top (CAMERA: GAME / PREVIEW / FLYING /
  PLAYING).
- Fly (F2 or the Fly button): arrow keys move, Page Up / Page Down go up /
  down, hold the right mouse button to look, mouse wheel = speed, Shift
  fast, Ctrl slow, + / - zoom. Fly stays on until you press F2 again: it
  keeps going while you edit, and comes back after you play.
  While flying your keys move the camera, not the car.
- F9 sets a key at the playhead, exactly what the camera shows (position,
  angle and zoom). The playhead then moves on by the key spacing, so: fly,
  F9, fly, F9 ... Click a key (or F3 / F4) to go back to it; F9 there
  updates it. F6 plays, F7 stops and gives the camera back.
- "Hide" clears the screen for filming; "Clean play" hides everything and
  plays from the start. F8 brings the menu back.
- Shots "Stay in place" (the camera keeps its spot in the world, even if the
  car drives off) or "Follow the car" (the camera rides along with it).
  Switch any time; the keys stay. Length, key spacing, ease in / out, a
  smooth or straight path, "Even speed". Undo / Redo, Library. "Key list"
  shows every key as a table.
- Timeline, like CapCut: click a shot to select it (white frame). Drag its
  left or right edge to trim it, drag the shot itself to move it before or
  after the others. "Split" (or right-click > Split at the playhead) cuts a
  shot in two; the camera move carries on smoothly across the cut. Keys
  trimmed off show greyed in the table. Mouse wheel zooms the timeline.
- Zoom: each key has a zoom (0.5x to 3x, the same as the phone's zoom).
  While flying, + / - zoom and F9 keys it. Otherwise the Zoom slider sets
  the selected key's zoom. You can also type it in the table.
- Tracks: put the playhead where something should happen and press
  "+ Keyframe". Every track has its own colour under the shots.
- Zoom track: "+ Keyframe > Zoom keyframe" uses the Zoom slider's value.
  The camera eases from the zoom before to the new one (time and curve in
  Settings). Select a Zoom keyframe and move the Zoom slider to change it.
  Before the first Zoom keyframe the keys' own zoom is used.
- Air suspension track: the 1st Air keyframe drops the car, the 2nd raises
  it, and so on. The lane draws the ride height (front solid, rear dashed).
  Settings: drop, raised heights, lowest point, time, axle order, height
  curve, and camber control (camber angles + camber curve) on or off.
  When the sequencer stops the car goes back to stock (or to the phone's
  air setting), unless you turn that off. Don't run Neptune's air ride at
  the same time. The phone's air remote shows SEQ while the sequencer
  drives the car.
- Settings (gear button in the menu) holds every option of each track and
  is saved with the sequence. Drag the dots of a curve graph to shape a
  move, or pick Smooth / Linear / Fast start / Slow start / Slam.
- Forzavista tracks (needs Forzavista running, see FORZA VISTA REMOTE): put
  the playhead where it should happen, "+ Keyframe" and pick e.g. Hood.
  The first Hood trigger opens it, the second closes it, the third opens it
  again, and so on. Each action has its own coloured track: a filled dot
  turns it on, a hollow dot turns it off. Drag a dot to move it,
  right-click it to delete. Scrub before a trigger and the car goes back to
  default. "Reset car" puts the car back to stock before a take; in
  Settings you can have Clean play do that for you every time.
  Forzavista key bindings can be any keys, even the sequencer's F-keys.
- When HorizonLens takes the camera, the game shows the driver's seat view
  for a moment: the camera module measures the car there before it can
  place the camera.

AIR SUSPENSION
- Settings (gear button) > Air Suspension. Off by default. Turn it on and an
  air remote appears on the camera screen: DOWN drops the car, UP raises it.
- All its settings are in the same place: ride height front/rear, drop
  amount, lowest point, speed, which axle moves first, and Air Camber (the
  wheels tilt in as the car drops, back to stock on the way up).
- Turning it off, changing car or closing HorizonLens puts the car back to
  stock. Don't run Neptune's air ride at the same time.

FORZA VISTA REMOTE
- Open doors, hood, trunk, roof, windows and more, and switch lights, from
  the phone. Uses Forzavista (a separate free app by HTTPJXRDN, from
  github.com/HTTPJXRDN/FH6-Forzavista) running next to the game.
- Settings (gear button) > Forza Vista. Off by default. Turn it on, pick
  which groups of buttons you want, and a car button appears on the camera
  screen that opens the remote.
- HorizonLens presses Forzavista's own key bindings. Tap "Set up key
  bindings for me" (with Forzavista closed) to give every action a spare
  key, then start Forzavista. Forza must be the active window on the PC.
- DRL colors, RGB, strobe, fade and presets aren't key-bindable in
  Forzavista, so use its own window for those.

POSITION TRACKING (ANDROID)
- On Android phones that support Google Play Services for AR, the camera
  also follows where you walk, lean and crouch, not only where you point.
  Chrome asks for the camera the first time: the rear camera is used for
  tracking only, nothing is recorded or sent.
- Point the back of the phone at a lit room with some detail (not a blank
  wall). Tap recenter to make where you stand the starting point.
- Turn it off or back on in the ... button > Tracking. Phones without AR
  support track rotation only.
- Movement Scale (... button > Tracking) sets how far the camera moves per
  metre you walk: 1.5x by default, up to 5x to cover more ground.
- Field of View (... button > Lens) sets how wide 1x zoom is. Raise it if
  the picture feels too zoomed in.

POSITION TRACKING (IPHONE APP)
- In Safari, iPhone tracks rotation only. The HorizonLens iPhone app (in the
  iPhone folder) adds ARKit, so the camera follows where you walk, lean
  and crouch too.
- Install it with Sideloadly and your Apple ID: see iPhone\INSTALL.txt.
  A free Apple ID install lasts 7 days, then install it again.
- Open the app, scan the QR code in HorizonLens, tap Tap to Start.

KNOWN LIMITS
- iPhone in Safari tracks rotation only (use the app for position).
- Recordings capture all PC sound, not only the game.
- A Forza update can break camera control until HorizonLens is updated.
- If zoom doesn't work, close HorizonLens, restart Forza, then start
  HorizonLens again.

REPORTING A PROBLEM
1. With HorizonLens still open (and right after the problem happened),
   double-click Self-Test.bat. It checks your PC and opens
   HorizonLens-selftest.txt.
2. Send that file, what you saw, your game edition (Steam / Microsoft
   Store), your graphics card and your phone model. For a recording problem,
   also send Videos\HorizonLens\.last-recording.log.
