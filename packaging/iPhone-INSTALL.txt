HorizonLens iPhone app - install with Sideloadly
================================================

The app adds position tracking (ARKit) on iPhone: the game camera follows
where you walk, lean and crouch, as well as where you point the phone.
Needs iOS 15 or newer (iPhone 6s and later).

INSTALL (on your Windows PC)
1. Install iTunes (from apple.com, not the Microsoft Store version) and
   Sideloadly from https://sideloadly.io
2. Plug the iPhone into the PC with a cable and tap Trust on the phone.
3. Open Sideloadly, drag HorizonLens-iPhone.ipa into it, type your Apple ID
   and press Start. Enter your Apple ID password / code if asked.
4. On the iPhone:
   - Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > tap your Apple ID > Trust
   - iOS 16 or newer: Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode > On
     (the iPhone restarts; confirm Turn On).

A free Apple ID install works for 7 days. After that the app won't open:
plug in and press Start in Sideloadly again (your settings are kept).

USE
1. On the PC: start HorizonLens and press Start.
2. On the iPhone: open HorizonLens and scan the QR code. Allow Camera
   (ARKit tracking only, nothing is recorded or sent) and Local Network.
3. Tap Tap to Start. In the ... button > Settings, Tracking shows
   "Position + rotation (AR)".
4. Point the back of the phone at a lit room. Tap recenter to set where you
   stand as the starting point.

If the app says it can't reach the PC: check HorizonLens is running and
Started, and the phone is on the same Wi-Fi. "Scan the QR code again" picks
up a new PC address.
