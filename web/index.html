Hide Button
Adds an eye button to the top left of the camera screen. Tap it to hide every button and control so only the picture is left; the phone still moves the camera and pinching still zooms. Tap it again to bring everything back.
Recording
Quality
Standard records the live view as it is. High and Max record the same shot through their own capture on the PC, much sharper: High at 1080×1920 (24 Mbps), Max at your game's full resolution (at least 1080×1920, 50 Mbps). The live view on this phone stays the same, so Wi-Fi isn't affected. For even more detail, run the game at a higher resolution (NVIDIA DSR / DLDSR). On PCs where that capture can't run, High and Max raise the live view's quality instead.

Locks the GAP camera where it's pointing now, so moving the phone no longer moves it and you can set the phone down and drive. The stick and HEIGHT still move it, and Recenter points it straight ahead. Only in Car View › GAP.

Dynamic Gap Cam
Zooms out the faster you go, easing smoothly. Past 0.5× the camera pulls back instead, since the game can't go wider. Pinch zoom sets the parked zoom.

Player Tracking
The locked camera turns to follow the nearest other player driving near you, then eases back to where you locked it. AI traffic and parked cars are ignored. Best in an online session.
